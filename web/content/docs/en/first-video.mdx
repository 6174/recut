After installing and starting the local service, going from opening the workspace to your first video takes a few steps.

## 1. Open the workspace

Visit https://app.recut.video. The browser detects and connects to your local service; if it can't, the workspace shows an install guide or lets you connect an existing remote service.

## 2. Meet the workspace

Five tabs run across the top: **Studio**, **Worlds**, **Projects**, **Assets** and **Apps**. A global Agent panel sits on the left for planning and executing creation with AI.

## 3. Import media

Open the media library and upload images, video or audio. Or describe your idea in the Studio composer and let AI help plan a video.

## 4. Pick a creation app and create a project

Studio lists your creation apps (AI Short Films, Remotion Video, Audio Studio and more). Click "New project," choose an app type, name the project, and enter the project workspace.

## 5. Finish and export

Arrange media, cut the timeline, add captions and voice. After previewing, export the finished video — the project stays local and you can keep editing anytime.

### Want to try AI creation right away?

- Paste a script into Studio and pick "Turn text into a video" to have AI plan and produce a short film.
- Editing talking-head content? Use Audio Studio to transcribe, clean fillers and dub.
- Batch-making thumbnails? Use Cover Studio to generate channel-sized covers.
