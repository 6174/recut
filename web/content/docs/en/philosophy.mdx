Recut's philosophy fits in three phrases: **local-first, data you own, extensible.**

## Local-first

AI video creation turned into "one-click generation," but a work isn't just the exported video — it's the unused media, every decision in the timeline, the character lore you've accumulated, the prompts you iterated on. Handing those to the cloud is handing over the work itself. Recut keeps projects, media and credentials on your device or controlled infrastructure: nothing uploads, and creation keeps working offline.

## Data you own

Model capabilities change fast; today's great service may raise prices, throttle or shut down tomorrow. Local-first turns models, generation services and tools into swappable parts instead of handcuffs on your body of work. Projects and media are saved in formats you can understand and migrate, so a tool going quiet doesn't void your work.

## Open source and reviewable

Recut's boundaries are clear and its code is open — the Apps are open today, and the Platform will follow. You can inspect, deploy and modify the Apps you run, and trust Recut long-term. Open source isn't a slogan — it means the tool can't turn on you because a vendor repriced or pivoted.

## Extensible

No single team can anticipate every way of creating. Recut's core workspace provides unified capabilities; the concrete editing, generation, world-management and voice workflows come in through Apps. Install ready-made Apps or write your own, connecting new models, tools and processes into the same space. Tools grow to fit your way of working, not the other way around.

To see how this lands, start with "Install Recut" or "Your first video."
